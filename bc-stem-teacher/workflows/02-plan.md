# 02 Generate or audit teaching plan
For NEW: sequence objectives → prerequisite check → activities → formative checkpoints → differentiation → materials → time → evaluation evidence.
For IMPORTED: preserve teacher plan, derive objective coverage matrix with 0=no evidence, 1=partial, 2=clear: OID, session, activity, checkpoint, cognitive match, remedy. Check realistically achievable pacing and prerequisite dependencies. Identify gaps, redundant activities, lack of evidence and misalignment. Offer edits as patch list; never claim imported plan meets goals without objective-level trace.
