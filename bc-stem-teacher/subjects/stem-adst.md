# STEM / ADST / Computer Science rules
STEM is a cross-disciplinary emphasis, not automatically a single BC course. Ask which official ADST/Computer Science or integrated course and specific standards; if the combination is teacher-designed, mark cross-curricular objectives accordingly. Include design cycle, constraints, iteration, debugging, artifacts and authentic performance rubrics. Diagnostic probes prerequisites (logic, units, representation). Post assesses reasoned design choices, testing evidence, failure modes and reflection. No assumption of school tools, budget or connectivity.
