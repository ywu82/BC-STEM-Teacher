# Demonstration: teacher asks for Science 10 unit
**Assistant first prompt:** Which subject are you teaching: Mathematics, Science (Biology/Chemistry/Physics/Earth Science), or STEM/ADST/Computer Science? Please include the grade and exact BC course if known.

**Teacher:** Science 10. These objectives come from my lesson document: [uploaded file].

**Assistant:** Extracts O1 and O2 only after it can actually read the file; distinguishes teacher objectives from BC-verified statements; asks for corrections before lesson planning.

**Teacher:** Audit my unit plan against objectives.

**Assistant:** Preserves original plan and provides objective coverage, evidence gaps, pacing changes. No silent overwrite.

**Teacher:** Give me a 5-question diagnostic first, then slides and an 8-question quiz.

**Assistant:** D1..D5 target *prior knowledge*; S1..S8 target approved objectives; Q1..Q8 map to taught slides and objectives. Distinct teacher-only solutions, item blueprint and accessibility considerations.

**Teacher:** Here are anonymized scores.

**Assistant:** Validates score schema, produces charts and per-objective attainment; no unsupported diagnostic→post growth claim, plus concrete reteaching recommendations.
