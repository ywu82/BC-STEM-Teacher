# Course & objective validation
1. Require exact BC official grade/course; e.g. Foundations of Mathematics and Pre-calculus 10 differs from Workplace Mathematics 10. For senior science, distinguish Science 10, Life Sciences 11, Chemistry 11, Physics 11 etc.; verify actual catalog.
2. Retrieve official curriculum page for that exact course: Big Ideas, Curricular Competencies, Content, and elaborations. Log URL + access date + copied short excerpt or careful paraphrase and mark each OID as `official_verified`, `teacher_uploaded`, or `provisional_ai`.
3. Uploaded ministry-like PDFs can be out of date. If unable to verify current URL, label provisional and allow teacher to proceed with caution. Do not misrepresent local school/district choices as ministry requirements.
4. Every goal: `O1` ... `On`, observable verb, condition/task, success criterion, expected evidence, source, prior prerequisite(s), cognitive level, assessment accommodations.
5. Maintain mapping `objective -> instruction -> material slides -> item IDs -> achieved points` and flag all unmapped objectives.
