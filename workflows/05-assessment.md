# 05 Instruction-based quiz OR test
Require approved objectives and teaching materials created OR imported. If not available, state provisional pending instructional source. Blueprint OIDs × slides × cognitive level × points; every tested question references material IDs and OID. Quiz: targeted, short, low-stakes, quick feedback. Test: broader coverage and multiple formats with suitable transfer/integration. Separate student sheet from teacher answer/rubric. Check answer key, sufficient objective sampling, no accidental answer leakage, accessible language, approved accommodations and science safety. Label question difficulty with justification and teacher-editable settings.
